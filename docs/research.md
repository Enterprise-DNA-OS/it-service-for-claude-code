These are judgments, not measurements. Private-price candidates receive a conservative bill score because their current annual invoice is unknown. Gainsight relies on customer-system signals. Qualio requires regulated quality-system validation beyond this scope. Search results for Freshservice alternatives include comparison articles and operator discussions; no keyword volume was measured. Complaints about asset charging are research leads, not product claims.

Freshservice adds internal service management, distinct from the existing HaloPSA build's managed-service billing. Contract management and product planning were built earlier tonight and excluded. The register holds incidents, service requests, services, assets, recurring problems and change decisions. It does not clone every Freshservice module.

Sources reopened this run:

- [Freshservice pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/pricing/): Starter US$19, Growth US$49 and Pro US$99 per agent per month, annual billing. Enterprise has custom pricing. Pro lists problem, change, major incident and release management. Additional asset units and functionality carry separate costs.
- [Freshservice ticket exports](https://support.freshservice.com/support/solutions/articles/50000006341-exporting-tickets): list-view CSV/Excel, selected fields, ticket-level records. Conversations and internal notes are excluded. Service-request item fields need a different export. More complete conversation history uses the API or account XML export.
- [Qualio pricing](https://www.qualio.com/pricing): tailored quote, implementation fee, regulated life-sciences workflow.
- [Gainsight pricing](https://www.gainsight.com/pricing/): request pricing, customer health and success workflow.

Do not state that Freshservice cannot answer our ten questions. Its reporting and custom analytics can overlap. Our advantage is inspectable joins, local ownership and rules the owner can change.
